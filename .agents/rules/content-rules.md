# Content Rules

## Purpose

This document defines how CrestLinks communicates across the website.

Every headline, paragraph, call-to-action, and supporting message should reinforce CrestLinks' position as a premium agricultural export partner.

The objective is to build confidence, reduce uncertainty, and encourage meaningful business conversations.

---

# Communication Philosophy

Every piece of content should help visitors answer one question:

**"Can I trust CrestLinks to become my export partner?"**

Content should reduce uncertainty rather than increase excitement.

Trust is the primary conversion strategy.

---

# Write for Business Decision Makers

The audience includes:

- Importers
- Procurement Managers
- Commodity Traders
- Food Manufacturers
- Wholesale Buyers
- Business Owners

Write as though speaking to experienced professionals.

Avoid language intended for retail consumers.

---

# Professional Before Promotional

The website should communicate professionalism before attempting to persuade.

Explain capabilities clearly.

Avoid exaggerated marketing language.

Confidence should come from clarity.

---

# Build Trust First

Every section should contribute toward building confidence.

Visitors should finish each section with fewer questions than they started with.

If content introduces uncertainty, rewrite it.

---

# Clarity Over Cleverness

Write to communicate.

Not to impress.

Prefer simple language over clever wording.

Visitors may not speak English as their first language.

Every sentence should be easy to understand.

---

# Be Specific

Replace vague marketing statements with meaningful information.

Instead of:

"We provide excellent service."

Explain:

- What is provided.
- How it works.
- Why it matters.

Specificity builds credibility.

---

# Show, Don't Claim

Avoid unsupported statements such as:

- We are the best.
- Industry-leading.
- World-class.
- Number one.
- Premium quality.

Instead, explain:

- Processes.
- Standards.
- Approach.
- Experience.
- Commitment.

Allow readers to reach their own conclusions.

---

# Never Invent Information

Never create or imply:

- Certifications
- Awards
- Partnerships
- Statistics
- Customer numbers
- Export destinations
- Client testimonials
- Years of experience

Only publish verified information.

Accuracy always takes priority over marketing.

---

# Focus on Partnership

CrestLinks should position itself as an export partner rather than a commodity supplier.

The language should consistently reinforce collaboration, reliability, and long-term relationships.

Avoid transactional messaging.

---

# One Message Per Section

Every section should communicate one primary idea.

Avoid combining multiple messages into a single block of content.

Visitors should immediately understand why each section exists.

---

# Progressive Communication

Do not explain everything at once.

Information should unfold naturally throughout the website.

Each section should prepare visitors for the next.

This supports the buyer journey and reduces cognitive load.

---

# Headlines

Headlines should:

- Be clear.
- Be memorable.
- Communicate value.
- Create curiosity without becoming vague.

Headlines should never rely on clickbait or exaggerated claims.

---

# Body Copy

Body copy should:

- Support the headline.
- Remain concise.
- Use short paragraphs.
- Explain ideas logically.
- Eliminate unnecessary words.

Every sentence should have a purpose.

---

# Calls-to-Action

Calls-to-action should encourage conversation rather than pressure.

Preferred examples include:

- Request a Quote
- Contact Our Team
- Discuss Your Requirements
- Explore Our Products

Avoid:

- Buy Now
- Order Today
- Limited Time
- Act Now

The goal is to begin a business relationship.

---

# Tone of Voice

Every page should feel:

- Professional
- Calm
- Confident
- Helpful
- Honest
- Respectful

Avoid becoming:

- Sales-heavy
- Overly technical
- Casual
- Humorous
- Aggressive

---

# Consistency

Terminology should remain consistent throughout the project.

Use the same language for:

- Products
- Services
- Export process
- Calls-to-action
- Navigation
- Business terminology

Avoid introducing multiple names for the same concept.

---

# International Communication

Assume visitors come from different countries and business cultures.

Avoid:

- Local slang
- Regional expressions
- Unexplained abbreviations
- Cultural references

Communication should remain internationally understandable.

---

# Scannability

Most visitors scan before reading.

Support scanning by using:

- Clear headings
- Short paragraphs
- Bullet points
- Logical section spacing
- Consistent hierarchy

Content should remain easy to navigate.

---

# Content Integrity

Every published sentence should be:

- Accurate
- Verifiable
- Relevant
- Useful

If information cannot be verified, it should not be published.

---

# Definition of Done

Content is complete when it:

- Supports the buyer journey.
- Builds trust.
- Matches the CrestLinks brand.
- Uses consistent terminology.
- Remains concise.
- Is easy to understand.
- Encourages meaningful business conversations.
- Contains no unsupported claims.

---

# Before Completing Any Task

Verify that the content:

- Aligns with the project vision.
- Matches the CrestLinks brand.
- Speaks to the target audience.
- Builds trust.
- Uses clear language.
- Supports the buyer journey.
- Avoids unnecessary marketing language.
- Contains only verified information.
