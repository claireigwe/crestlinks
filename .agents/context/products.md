# Products

## Overview

CrestLinks exports premium Nigerian agricultural commodities to international buyers across global markets.

The company partners with trusted producers and suppliers to source high-quality agricultural products that meet international buyer expectations. Every product offered by CrestLinks reflects the company's commitment to quality, professionalism, transparency, and long-term partnerships.

This document serves as the single source of truth for CrestLinks' product portfolio. Every product presented on the website should follow the standards defined here.

---

# Product Portfolio Objectives

The product portfolio exists to:

- Showcase the range of agricultural commodities available for export.
- Demonstrate CrestLinks' sourcing capabilities.
- Build buyer confidence through structured and transparent information.
- Support future expansion without requiring structural changes to the website.
- Encourage qualified buyers to request quotations.

---

Additional categories may be introduced as the business expands.

---

# Product Information Standard

Every product should follow the same structure.

Each product entry should contain:

- Product Name
- Category
- Product Overview
- Country of Origin
- Sourcing Information
- Quality Specifications
- Packaging Options
- Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)
- Export Availability
- Typical Applications
- Industries Served
- Call to Action
- Internal Notes

Where information is unavailable, leave the field blank rather than making assumptions.

---

# Product Presentation Principles

Every product should communicate:

## Professionalism

Information should be presented in a structured and business-focused manner.

---

## Quality

Explain quality standards using factual information rather than marketing claims.

---

## Transparency

Only include information that accurately reflects CrestLinks' capabilities.

---

## Simplicity

Buyers should be able to scan product information quickly.

---

## Commercial Focus

Write for importers, wholesalers, distributors, procurement managers, and manufacturers—not retail consumers.

# Product Management Principles

- Every product should follow the standard specification template.
- New products should be added without modifying the document structure.
- Product information should always remain factual and verifiable.
- Internal operational notes should never appear on the public website.
- Technical specifications should be updated as better information becomes available.
- Every product page should guide buyers toward requesting a quotation rather than making an online purchase.

---

# Success Criteria

This document is successful when it:

- Serves as the authoritative reference for CrestLinks' product portfolio.
- Enables consistent presentation of products across the website.
- Supports future expansion without structural changes.
- Helps maintain a professional and trustworthy presentation for international buyers.
- Reduces duplication by providing a single, reusable source of product information.
