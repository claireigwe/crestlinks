# Sitemap

## Overview

The CrestLinks website is a corporate B2B marketing website designed to establish credibility, communicate the company's capabilities, and generate qualified export enquiries from international buyers.

The website should remain simple, intuitive, and focused on helping visitors quickly understand who CrestLinks is, what the company exports, and how to begin a business conversation.

The architecture should prioritise clarity over complexity.

---

# Navigation Structure

The primary navigation consists of:

- Home
- About
- Products
- Export Services
- Contact

The navigation should remain consistent across all pages.

# Page Objectives

## Home

### Purpose

Introduce CrestLinks, establish credibility, showcase the company's capabilities, and guide visitors towards requesting a quotation.

### Primary Audience

First-time visitors.

### Primary CTA

Request a Quote

---

## About

### Purpose

Introduce the company, explain its mission, values, and positioning, and strengthen buyer confidence.

### Primary CTA

Contact Our Team

---

## Products

### Purpose

Present CrestLinks' agricultural commodity portfolio in a structured catalogue format.

### Primary CTA

Request a Quote

---

## Export Services

### Purpose

Explain how CrestLinks manages sourcing, quality assurance, documentation, logistics coordination, and export operations.

The objective is to demonstrate professionalism and reduce buyer uncertainty.

### Primary CTA

Discuss Your Requirements

---

## Contact

### Purpose

Provide multiple channels through which buyers can begin business discussions.

The page should encourage qualified enquiries rather than casual contact.

### Primary CTA

Request a Quote

---

# Homepage Structure

The homepage should tell a complete story while encouraging further exploration.

Recommended section order:

1. Hero
2. Trust Indicators
3. About CrestLinks
4. Featured Commodities
5. Why Choose CrestLinks
6. Export Services
7. Our Export Process
8. Industries We Serve
9. Call to Action

Every section should naturally lead visitors towards requesting a quotation.

# Internal Linking Strategy

Pages should reinforce one another.

Examples:

Home → Products

Home → About

Products → Contact

Export Services → Contact

About → Products

Contact should remain accessible from every page.

---

# Call-to-Action Strategy

The primary conversion goal is generating qualified business enquiries.

Preferred CTAs include:

- Request a Quote
- Contact Our Team
- Discuss Your Requirements
- Explore Our Products

Every page should include at least one meaningful call to action.

---

# Future Expansion

The sitemap should support future additions without major restructuring.

Potential future pages include:

- Industries
- Quality Assurance
- Certifications
- Sustainability
- Frequently Asked Questions
- News & Insights

These pages are intentionally excluded from Version 1 to maintain focus and support the two-day development timeline.

---

# Out of Scope

The sitemap does not include:

- E-commerce
- Product checkout
- Customer accounts
- Order tracking
- Inventory search
- Blog
- Career pages
- Customer portal
- Multi-language support

These features may be considered in future versions if business requirements change.
