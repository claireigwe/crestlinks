# Content

## Overview

This document defines the content architecture and approved website copy for CrestLinks.

It serves as the single source of truth for all user-facing content and establishes the messaging hierarchy across the website.

This document does not replace the business documentation.

- Company information is maintained in `company.md`.
- Product information is maintained in `products.md`.
- Brand messaging is defined in `brand.md`.
- Business goals are defined in `vision.md`.

This document transforms those resources into a clear, consistent website experience.

---

# Content Principles

Every piece of content should:

- Build trust before attempting to sell.
- Be written for international B2B buyers.
- Be concise and easy to scan.
- Prioritise clarity over creativity.
- Support the buyer's decision-making process.
- Encourage conversation rather than immediate conversion.

The website should never overwhelm visitors with unnecessary information.

# Homepage

## Story Goal

The homepage should answer one question:

**"Why should I trust CrestLinks as my export partner?"**

By the end of the page, visitors should understand:

- Who CrestLinks is.
- What the company exports.
- Why the company is different.
- How the export process works.
- How to begin a business conversation.

# Content Governance

## Writing Standards

All website copy should:

- Reflect the CrestLinks brand.
- Maintain a professional B2B tone.
- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Be easy to scan.
- Use active voice.
- Remain internationally understandable.
- Focus on building trust before selling.
