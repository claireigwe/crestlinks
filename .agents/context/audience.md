# Target Audience

## Overview

CrestLinks serves business customers looking to source premium agricultural commodities from Nigeria for commercial use.

The website is designed for decision-makers involved in international procurement, sourcing, manufacturing, wholesale distribution, and commodity trading.

Visitors are not browsing casually. They arrive with a business objective, evaluate multiple suppliers, and are looking for a reliable export partner capable of meeting their sourcing requirements.

The website should provide the information they need to assess CrestLinks as a credible business partner.

---

# Primary Audience

## International Importers

Businesses that purchase agricultural commodities for resale or distribution within their local markets.

### Primary Goals

- Find a reliable export partner.
- Source quality agricultural commodities.
- Reduce sourcing risks.
- Build long-term supplier relationships.

### Key Questions

- Can CrestLinks supply the products I need?
- Can they be trusted?
- Do they understand international exports?
- How do I request a quotation?
- Can they meet my quality expectations?

---

## Food Manufacturers

Companies that purchase agricultural commodities as raw materials for food production.

### Primary Goals

- Secure consistent product supply.
- Maintain quality standards.
- Build dependable sourcing partnerships.

### Key Questions

- What products are available?
- Can quality specifications be met?
- Can supply remain consistent over time?
- What is the export process?

---

## Wholesalers & Distributors

Businesses purchasing agricultural commodities in bulk for resale.

### Primary Goals

- Source quality products.
- Find dependable suppliers.
- Establish long-term commercial relationships.

### Key Questions

- What products are available?
- What quantities can CrestLinks supply?
- How does the ordering process work?
- How do I begin discussions?

---

# Secondary Audience

## Commodity Traders

Professionals connecting buyers and suppliers across international markets.

They are interested in:

- Product availability
- Reliability
- Export capability
- Business responsiveness

---

## Procurement Professionals

Professionals responsible for supplier evaluation.

Their priority is reducing business risk.

They evaluate suppliers based on professionalism, transparency, documentation, communication, and perceived operational capability.

---

# Audience Characteristics

Most visitors are:

- Business professionals.
- Experienced decision-makers.
- Time conscious.
- Detail oriented.
- Comparing multiple suppliers.
- Seeking long-term partnerships rather than one-off purchases.

Many visitors may never have worked with a Nigerian exporter before, making trust one of the website's primary objectives.

---

# Buyer Concerns

Visitors are primarily trying to reduce uncertainty.

Common concerns include:

- Product quality
- Supplier reliability
- Export experience
- Communication
- Documentation
- Delivery expectations
- Professionalism
- Transparency

The website should proactively address these concerns wherever possible.

---

# Information Buyers Expect

Visitors should quickly find answers to questions such as:

- Who is CrestLinks?
- What products are available?
- How does sourcing work?
- How does the export process work?
- Why should I trust CrestLinks?
- How do I request a quotation?
- How do we begin working together?

The website should minimise the effort required to locate this information.

---

# Decision Drivers

Buyers are most influenced by:

- Professional presentation.
- Clear communication.
- Transparency.
- Product quality.
- Confidence in the export process.
- Ease of contacting the company.
- Responsiveness.
- Long-term partnership potential.

Price is important, but trust is often the deciding factor when selecting a new supplier.

---

# User Journey

Most visitors will follow a similar journey.

1. Discover CrestLinks.
2. Evaluate credibility.
3. Review products.
4. Understand the export process.
5. Assess trustworthiness.
6. Request a quotation or initiate contact.

Every page should support this journey.

---

# Website Expectations

Visitors expect the website to be:

- Professional.
- Easy to navigate.
- Informative.
- Fast.
- Mobile friendly.
- Visually credible.
- Business focused.

The website should reinforce the impression that CrestLinks operates to international business standards.

---

# Success Criteria

The audience document is successful when it helps every design, content, and development decision answer one question:

**"Will this make an international buyer more confident in doing business with CrestLinks?"**

If the answer is yes, the decision aligns with the needs of the target audience.
