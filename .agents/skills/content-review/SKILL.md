# Content Review

## Objective

Review all user-facing content to ensure it aligns with the CrestLinks brand, supports the buyer journey, and communicates clearly with international B2B audiences.

This skill focuses on communication quality rather than visual design or technical implementation.

The objective is to ensure every word builds trust.

---

# When to Use

Use this skill after:

- Creating a new page.
- Updating page copy.
- Writing headlines.
- Writing calls-to-action.
- Adding new sections.
- Revising existing content.

Do not use this skill for code reviews.

---

# Required Context

Review:

- brand.md
- audience.md
- content.md
- company.md
- products.md
- content-rules.md

Execute the Company Understanding skill before beginning.

---

# Preconditions

Before reviewing, confirm:

- The content exists.
- Business information has been verified.
- The intended audience is known.
- The page objective is understood.

---

# Workflow

## Step 1 — Review Purpose

Identify:

- Why this content exists.
- What business objective it supports.
- What the reader should understand after reading it.

If the objective is unclear, revise the content.

---

## Step 2 — Review Brand Alignment

Confirm the content reflects the CrestLinks brand.

The tone should feel:

- Professional
- Calm
- Confident
- Helpful
- Honest

---

## Step 3 — Review Audience Fit

Review from the perspective of an international buyer.

Ask:

- Is the language internationally understandable?
- Does this answer the buyer's questions?
- Does it reduce uncertainty?
- Would this build confidence?

---

## Step 4 — Review Clarity

Check for:

- Long sentences
- Repetition
- Unnecessary words
- Ambiguous statements
- Marketing clichés

Every sentence should communicate one idea clearly.

---

## Step 5 — Verify Accuracy

Confirm:

- No unsupported claims.
- No invented certifications.
- No fabricated statistics.
- No inaccurate business information.
- No misleading wording.

When uncertain, remove or verify.

---

## Step 6 — Review Calls-to-Action

Ensure every CTA:

- Feels natural.
- Matches the page objective.
- Encourages conversation.
- Supports the buyer journey.

Avoid aggressive sales language.

---

## Step 7 — Improve

Rewrite wherever necessary.

Prefer:

- Greater clarity.
- Greater specificity.
- Stronger trust.
- Simpler language.

---

# Validation Checklist

Confirm:

✓ Brand voice is consistent.

✓ Audience needs are addressed.

✓ Content builds trust.

✓ Information is accurate.

✓ Headlines communicate value.

✓ CTAs feel natural.

✓ Language remains concise.

✓ No unnecessary repetition exists.

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Writing for consumers.
- Marketing hype.
- Unsupported claims.
- Generic business language.
- Long paragraphs.
- Keyword stuffing.
- Multiple ideas in one section.

---

# Completion Criteria

Content is complete when:

- It supports the business objective.
- It builds confidence.
- It is easy to understand.
- It reflects the CrestLinks brand.
- It encourages meaningful business conversations.
- Every sentence has a clear purpose.
